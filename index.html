From plan to answers in three steps

  1. 1

    Upload your floor plan

    A PNG, JPG or PDF. Click two points to set the scale, like the ends of a wall you know.

  2. 2

    AI builds the model

    It places entrances, lines, counters, seating, walls and doors, and asks you for the numbers the plan can't show.

  3. 3

    Simulate and improve

    Watch the day play out, see waits and bottlenecks, and test AI's fixes, from one more teller to a better layout.

Everything you need to fix a queue

Reads your real plan

AI turns the drawing into blocks, links, walls and doorways. Walking paths go around walls, through doors.

A real simulation

Thirty replications of the day with 95% confidence ranges: waits, line lengths, utilization, people leaving.

AI review, tested

The AI explains the bottleneck and proposes fixes. Each one is simulated, so you see measured before and after.

Layout scorecard

Grades flow order, crossing paths, queue space at the peak and doors kept clear, then suggests a better arrangement.

Animated replay

See people walk, queue and get served over your plan, at any speed, at any moment of the day.

AI pictures

Turn the layout into a realistic illustration for your slides, before and after a change.

Numbers you can trust

The AI reads, explains and suggests. It never decides the numbers: every result comes from a tested simulation engine, and every AI suggestion is simulated before you see it.

  • 100+automated tests on Windows, macOS and Linux
